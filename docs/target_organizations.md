# Target Organizations Research & Identification

> **Academic & Professional Outreach Context**  
> Prepared as part of the SafeX Solutions Internship Program (Week 4, Group 56). Identifies real public organizations in the Islamabad/Rawalpindi area representing small restaurant partners, foodtech incubators, and SME business advisors.

---

## Target Organization 1: Islamabad Chamber of Commerce & Industry (ICCI) — SME Committee

- **Organization Name:** Islamabad Chamber of Commerce & Industry (ICCI) — Small & Medium Enterprises (SME) Committee
- **Overview:** Primary trade organization representing over 5,000 commercial enterprises, independent food vendors, and retail merchants across the Islamabad Capital Territory.
- **Strategic Fit:** Represents local restaurant partners and retail food vendors operating on delivery platforms (such as Careem NOW) who require empirical demand forecasting frameworks to optimize inventory and operational planning.
- **Public Contact Channel:** `icci@icci.com.pk` | [ICCI Official Contact Page](https://www.icci.com.pk/contact-us) | [ICCI LinkedIn Profile](https://www.linkedin.com/company/islamabad-chamber-of-commerce-&-industry)
- **Verified Source URL:** [https://www.icci.com.pk](https://www.icci.com.pk)

## Target Organization 2: National Incubation Center (NIC) Islamabad — FoodTech & Retail Incubator

- **Organization Name:** National Incubation Center (NIC) Islamabad — FoodTech & Retail Acceleration Wing
- **Overview:** Pakistan's premier technology startup incubator, managed by LMKT in collaboration with Ignite, supporting foodtech, dark kitchens, and retail logistics ventures.
- **Strategic Fit:** Mentors early-stage delivery-first restaurant startups and dark kitchen operators in Rawalpindi/Islamabad seeking data science tools to model peak demand spikes and optimize kitchen prep SLAs.
- **Public Contact Channel:** `info@nicpakistan.pk` | [NIC Pakistan Contact](https://nicpakistan.pk/contact-us/) | [NIC Pakistan LinkedIn Profile](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nicpakistan/)
- **Verified Source URL:** [https://nicpakistan.pk](https://nicpakistan.pk)

## Target Organization 3: Rawalpindi Chamber of Commerce and Industry (RCCI) — Retail & Hospitality Wing

- **Organization Name:** Rawalpindi Chamber of Commerce and Industry (RCCI) — Retail & Hospitality Wing
- **Overview:** Established business body representing over 8,000 commercial merchants, food service establishments, and hospitality businesses across Rawalpindi district.
- **Strategic Fit:** Provides business guidance to independent food merchants looking to stabilize operating margins, reduce food waste, and coordinate promotional campaigns around seasonal demand shifts.
- **Public Contact Channel:** `info@rcci.org.pk` | [RCCI Contact Portal](https://rcci.org.pk/contact-us/) | [RCCI Official Website](https://rcci.org.pk)
- **Verified Source URL:** [https://rcci.org.pk](https://rcci.org.pk)
